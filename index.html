Day 2

Polignano & the Cliff Dinner

The full Polignano experience: sea caves, whitewashed alleyways, raw seafood platters — and dinner inside a cave carved into the Adriatic cliffs.

Morning
Optional
🍝
Orecchiette Pasta Class — Bari Vecchia
Catch the early train to Bari (30 mins) for a hands-on pasta class with a local nonna in the old town lanes of Via dell’Arco Basso. Watch the traditional ear-shaped pasta shaped by hand, then eat what you made. Back on the train to Polignano by midday.
OPTIONAL ADD-ONBOOK VIA GETYOURGUIDE
Midday

Train to Polignano a Mare — 30 mins from Fasano, under €5. Enter through the Arco Marchesale into the whitewashed old town. Walk Via Commedia delle Mura where poetry is painted onto walls and doors, then find the terrace views at Terrazza Santo Stefano and Ponte Borbonico.

Afternoon

Walk down to Lama Monachile (Cala Porta) — the iconic pebble cove between two limestone cliffs crossed by the old Roman bridge. This is the photo. If time allows, book a 90-minute sea cave boat tour passing Grotta Azzurra and Grotta Arcivescovado. Sunset drink at Terrazza Monachile Bar overlooking the bay.

Grotta Palazzese — Cave Restaurant
Book 4–6 weeks ahead for May. Book direct at grottapalazzese.it — third-party sites add a surcharge. Request a cliff-edge table in the booking comments (not guaranteed but worth asking). Dinner begins at 18:00; a 19:30 seating gives you the best of daylight turning to dusk. The cave setting is genuinely spectacular — you’re inside a sea cave carved into the cliffside, with the Adriatic below.
BOOK DIRECTREQUEST CLIFF TABLE
Budget approx. €250–300 per person with wine. The food is good, not exceptional — you are paying for the setting. Worth every euro as a bucket-list experience.
Insider Note
After dinner you can linger in the cave — there’s no strict rush once the last seating is done. The walk back through the illuminated old town at night is magical. Plenty of taxis from the main square back to your masseria, or pre-arrange with your hotel.

Your chill day option for Leg 1. The trulli tour is brilliant and worth doing — but if Day 2 in Polignano was big, there’s no shame in a pool day, a slow lunch at the masseria, and a late wander. Both options below.

Evening

Either way: a quiet evening back at the masseria before the move south tomorrow. Pool, aperitivo, an early-ish night. Last breakfast on the farm in the morning.

🏠 Alberobello — What to Know

The trulli zone (Rione Monti) is genuinely unlike anywhere in Europe — over 1,500 stone cone-roofed houses, many still lived in. Busier in summer but fine in May. Buy nothing from the first stall you see; the ceramics and local products improve the further into the lanes you go.

Day 4

Move South — Into the Baroque

Check out of the masseria and take the train south to Lecce — the golden Baroque city that rewards slow wandering, excellent aperitivo, and some of the best pasta in Italy.

Morning
Check-out

Final masseria breakfast (eat well — the farm produce is extraordinary). Transfer to Fasano or Brindisi station for the southbound train. Journey takes about 1h 15m to Lecce.

FROM ~€11 ON TRENITALIA
Afternoon

Check in and go straight into the old town. Piazza del Duomo is the centrepiece — the cathedral, the Bell Tower and the Seminary form one of the most complete Baroque ensembles in southern Italy. Then the Basilica di Santa Croce, whose facade is a riot of carved figures that took 150 years to complete. Walk the back lanes: Via Vittorio Emanuele, the Roman amphitheatre in Piazza Sant’Oronzo.

Day 5

Caribbean Blue — Salento Beaches

Your chill day for Leg 2. The Salento coastline in May: impossibly clear turquoise water, white sand, no crowds, unhurried lunch somewhere with your feet in the sand. Leave the agenda at the hotel.

Punta
Prosciutto
🏖
Punta Prosciutto
Wide pristine white sand, flat turquoise water that genuinely looks Caribbean, backed by a pine forest. In May the car park is nowhere near full and the water is warm enough to swim. Designated a protected coastal zone — no development visible from the beach.

Getting there without a car: Pre-book a taxi from Lecce (~45 mins, ~€40 each way) or join a beach day tour from Lecce (several operators run these in summer — check GetYourGuide or Viator closer to the date). If staying at Don Totu, they’re 12 mins from this coast anyway — the Vespas are an option.

On the Adriatic side: dramatic limestone faraglioni (sea stacks) rising from crystal water, natural rock pools, and a tiny fishing village. More dramatic scenery than Punta Prosciutto, less swimming-friendly sand. Both are about the same distance from Lecce — pick one, or do a morning at one and afternoon at the other.

Day 6

Primitivo & Negroamaro — Then One Last Dinner

A full wine day through Manduria: two Primitivo estates, a traditional lunch, back by mid-afternoon. Then the farewell dinner Puglia deserves.

Full Day
Tour
🍷
Primitivo & Negroamaro Wine Tour from Lecce
Guided tour to two wineries in the Manduria DOC zone. Cellar tours, barrel tastings, traditional Apulian lunch with the wines. Returns to Lecce mid-afternoon — enough time to freshen up before dinner.
LUNCH INCLUDEDBOOK VIA TRIPADVISORPRIMITIVO DI MANDURIA DOC
What to Look For
Primitivo di Manduria — full-bodied, plummy, 14–16% ABV. Often aged in French and American oak. Try vintage 2019 or 2020 if available — outstanding years.

Negroamaro — darker, more tannic, earthy and savoury. Often blended with Malvasia Nera. A great contrast in the tastings.
Farewell
Dinner
🍽
Primo Restaurant — Michelin Starred
Chef Solaika Marrocco’s Lecce restaurant is the finest meal you’ll have in Puglia. Seasonal Salentine ingredients, exceptional wine list, and a room that feels like a proper occasion. Book well ahead — this fills weeks in advance for May. End with a final slow walk through the illuminated Baroque centro storico. End of the line in the best possible way.
